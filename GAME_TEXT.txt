#  HOW TO EDIT:

#   * To REMOVE a piece of text, delete everything after the "="  (e.g.  pause.text = )
#   * Words in {curly braces} are filled in by the game, like {floor} or {number}.

#   * **Double stars** make words bold — only works on lines marked "(bold ok)".
#   * Lines starting with # are just notes. Lines in [ brackets ] are just headings.
#   * Save this file, then reopen the game (or use Game > Reload Game) to see changes.
